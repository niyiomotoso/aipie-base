# CTA (Call to Action) Patterns

> Extracted from 46 past scripts. These are Niyi's proven CTA structures.

## Pattern 1: Comment + DM Offer (Most Common)
Offer something valuable in exchange for a comment keyword.
- "Comment AI and I'll send it to you."
- "Comment guide and I will send you a detailed note on this with practical examples."
- "Comment context and I'll send it to you."
- "If you want the links to all 10 courses, just comment AI and I'll DM them to you."
- "If you want the prompts I'm using in this video, just comment context and I'll send it to you."
- "Now, if you need more information about these platforms and their age recommendations, just comment Tech and I will DM it to you."

## Pattern 2: Follow + Next Video Tease
Promise related content coming next.
- "In my next video, I'll talk about my recommended AI courses for leadership, business and management professionals. So make sure you follow me and see you on the next one."
- "In the next part, I will share some platforms where kids can take these skills to the next level. So make sure you follow me and see you on the next one."
- "So follow me for more content and see you in my next video."

## Pattern 3: Simple Sign-Off
Clean, brief close.
- "See you in my next video."
- "Follow me for more AI tips."
- "Follow me for more AI pro tips."
- "Follow me for more AI processes."

## Pattern 4: Identity + Follow
Introduce himself for new viewers, then ask for follow.
- "My name is Niyi. I'm a lead software engineer with experience building AI systems. And on this feed, I share information like this to help you improve your business and career using AI and tech. So follow me for more content and see you in my next video."
- "And if you're seeing me for the first time, my name is Niyi..."

## Pattern 5: Question to the Audience
End by prompting engagement.
- "What do you guys think? See you in my next video."
- "So which AI platform do you find most useful at the moment?"
- "Have you tried these code editors?"

## CTA Structure Rules
- CTAs are always the LAST thing in the script
- Usually 1-2 sentences
- Almost always includes "follow me" or "see you in my next video"
- Comment keyword CTAs use simple words: "AI", "guide", "tools", "context", "Tech", "academy"
- The DM offer always promises something specific and valuable (links, guides, details)
- Never pushy or desperate — always casual and helpful

## CTA Formula
```
[Offer: what they get] + [Action: comment keyword] + [Follow ask] + [Sign-off]
```
Example: "If you want the exact steps, comment AI and I'll send it to you. Follow me for more, and see you in my next video."
