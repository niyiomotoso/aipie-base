Topic: 4 Beginner-Friendly AI Learning Platforms (Fundamentals)

There are four AI learning platforms I will recommend to people who want to learn AI at its core. You know, whether you're a student or a young professional or you're already deep in your career, AI is now becoming part of our everyday. Now, you don't necessarily need to become technical, but at least you should understand some of the fundamentals. So save this video or share it with someone that might find it useful. The first website is Element of AI. Everything about this platform is beginner-friendly and it's available in more than 20 languages. One thing I like about their course is how it explains the AI concepts to you without asking you to be technical. The second platform is SkillsBuild by IBM. It's a very awesome platform. It's less theory and it focuses more on how AI actually affects your work. They give very practical courses. They focus on how AI affects real jobs, real workplaces and real life decisions. And it also comes with a free certificate at the end, which doesn't hurt if you're trying to build your profile. This third one is by University of Maryland and it's called Artificial Intelligence and Career Development. I like the frame in here because it's not just about learning AI, it's about learning how AI is changing different industries and your career path. And of course, it comes with a certificate at the end. This fourth one is Career Essentials in Generative AI and it's by Microsoft and LinkedIn. I really like what LinkedIn did with this thing. They created what employers are asking job candidates about using AI in the workplace and they built the course off it. So yes, it realistically prepares you on how to apply AI at work.
