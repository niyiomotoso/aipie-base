Topic: AI SUBSETS 5

This fifth one is my favorite, and it is generative AI. From the name, you already know the
meaning. It's about generating stuff. I like it because it's not just about analyzing data. It's about
generating things from scratch. You see those funny videos of Cristiano Ronaldo performing at a
concert or UJZ playing a football match? Those are products of Gen AI. As a matter of fact, the
ultimate goal of Gen AI is that you imagine something, you prompt the machine, and it gives you
the results. I should also mention that it's not always those complex applications. Even those
simple utilities, like when you prompt ChatGPT to write an essay for you on a particular topic, it's
still Gen AI. So it's all about unlocking creativity, and you see it in every industry nowadays, from
art to music to content creation to marketing. You see generative AI. Of course, there are other
subsets as well, like robotics, like evolutionary algorithms, and even reinforcement learning. I
like that one too, because you see people building agents that can beat humans at games like
chess. Also, it is important that we recognize that these technologies we are discussing might
be using just one or a combination of these subsets. Take, for example, Alexa, the voice
assistant. It's at least a combination of machine learning, deep learning, and natural language
processing.
