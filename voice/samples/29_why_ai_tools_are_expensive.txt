Topic: WHY AI TOOLS ARE EXPENSIVE

AI tools are way too expensive. Even as someone that builds AI systems every day, I completely
agree. Two major reasons for this, I'll share them with you and tell you what you can do to
reduce costs from your end. You see, behind every chatbot, every image generation tool, every
video generation tool, is a server farm somewhere, burning electricity and crunching data 24/7.
This processing power costs millions. Someone has to pay. Number two reason, we have paid
for years of research and investments that have gone into AI. I'm talking about years of research
and development that companies have poured billions of dollars into. And now it's time to get
their money back because AI isn't just tech, it's also business. But here is the good news. Just
like the way mobile phones were very expensive in the late 90s and early 2000s, then over time,
the cost dropped. The exact same thing will happen in the AI industry because more parties will
come into the game and it will force the cost to drop. So what can you do right now? Number
one, use the free tiers as much as you can. Almost every AI tool has a free plan or there's a free
alternative somewhere. So try to look for those ones and make use of them. Number two is for
AI engineers and people that build AI systems. For example, instead of rushing to Amazon
Bedrock to host your models, why not try the open source LMTD first? Or instead of running
automation on Make.com, you can try editing.io. So you don't need to worry about cost every
time. You just need to know where to go. If you type tools in the comments, I'll send you a list of
my favorite AI tools.
