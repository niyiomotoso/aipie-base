ChatGPT can lie to you, and if you're not careful, it will land you in big trouble. It is called AI
hallucination. I will give you some examples on how you can fix it. Hallucination is when
ChatGPT makes up answers that sound legit, but they are completely false. It could be in form
of fake data, fake sources, or references that don't even exist at all. For example, you ask
ChatGPT to help you rewrite your CV, and then it goes ahead to add that you led a NASA space
mission in 2019, and you've never worked in NASA in your life, but AI has made it sound so
real. That's hallucination. Another example, you ask ChatGPT, how can I open a GTBank
account? And it says, step one, download the mobile app. Step two, enter your social security
number. That doesn't make sense, Abhi, because we don't use social security number in
Nigeria. But the stupid ChatGPT can still tell you that. So why does this hallucination happen?
It's because ChatGPT is not a fact-checker in the traditional sense. It's more of a guide that is
very good at guessing what the right answer is. Let me tell you two things you can do to fix this.
Number one, every time you use ChatGPT to do something very important, always ask for
sources. If it can't back it up, don't trust it. Number two, when you're asking these questions, be
very, very specific. If your question is not clear, it will confuse the AI. The clearer your question
is, the lesser the chances of ChatGPT lying to you. I made a free guide that will teach you
