Topic: WHY AI RESPONDS SLOWLY TO YOUR MESSAGE

ChatGPT is responding slowly to your messages. Let me tell you why and two things you can
do about it. The main reason for this is that you've been chatting in the same thread for a long
time, and this forces ChatGPT to slow down drastically. See, every time you send a message in
ChatGPT, it reviews the entire message in the thread to give you the right response. ChatGPT
doesn't have memory in the traditional sense. Every time you send a message, it needs to
establish the context all over again before sending you a response. So if that conversation has
continued for a long time, the response time will be reduced. It doesn't matter if your account is
free or you are paying for the account. This affects everyone. The number one way to fix this,
always start a new chat for every fresh conversation. You will immediately notice that the
response time is fast. Number two, there are times where you genuinely need to have long
conversations, like when you're using ChatGPT to work on a project, maybe at work or in
school. Simply ask ChatGPT to save the important details in memory, then open a new chat and
ask ChatGPT to recollect those details. You will immediately notice again that the response time
would have improved. Here is a bonus tip. For this memory referencing to work, you need to go
to settings, memory, and turn on this flag, reference saved memories. That way, if you ask
ChatGPT to recollect anything that has saved in memory, it will always do that in a new chat,
and you don't have to worry about these slow responses again. If you're new here...
