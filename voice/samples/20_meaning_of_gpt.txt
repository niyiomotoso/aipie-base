Topic: MEANING OF GPT

GPT is actually an acronym for Generative Pre-trained Transformer. I know that sounds like a
robot's full name, so let me explain. Generative means it's able to create those textual and
visual responses that you see. Pre-trained is talking about the fact that it already has a very
wide knowledge base. In fact, when I was going through the white paper for GPT-3, the raw
training set was about 45 terabytes in plain text. And lastly, Transformer explains the ability of
the model to take those prompts, process it in a very smart way, and also focus on the parts that
matter. And this transformer is the brain behind everything we do with ChatGPT. The thing is,
the different versions of GPTs that we use now, even though they are very powerful, they are
just the beginning. We are looking at a future where these transformers are able to do things
that our individual brains can't even compete. So will the transformer take your job? Maybe,
unless you learn how to use it.
