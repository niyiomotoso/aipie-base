Topic: HOW TO REMOVE EM-DASHES

You can now get rid of em-dashes completely in your ChatGPT responses by just doing this one
simple tweak. The interesting part is that Sam Altman, the CEO of OpenAI has also worked with
it. And if you don't know what that means, it is the number one indicator that your text is AI
generated by just looking at it. So here's what you do. Go to ChatGPT and under
personalization, you will see custom instructions. You will paste this instruction there telling
ChatGPT to stop using em-dashes. Then save. That's it. ChatGPT will stop using M-dashes in
the responses it gives to you.
