Topic: CHATGPT SETTINGS FOR BETTER PERFORMANCE

Turn on these three ChatGPT settings now to make it 10 times more powerful. Number one, use
GPT-5 in thinking mode. This will make ChatGPT think longer for better answers and it makes
fewer mistakes. Number two, go to your settings, personalization, and turn on custom
instructions. There you can tell it who you are and how you like your replies. Now, every
conversation will be tailored to your role, your tone, and your preferred format. Number three, go
to memory under personalization and turn on those two memory options. Once you do that,
ChatGPT will always remember your preferences and pull context from your past chats. That
way, you don't have to repeat yourself every time.
