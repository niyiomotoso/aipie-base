Topic: My First Driverless Waymo Ride (LA to West Hollywood)

I took my first ever driverless car ride from downtown Los Angeles to West Hollywood. This is a Waymo ride, and just like Uber and Lyft, everything starts from the app, and honestly, the experience is far better. For example, Waymo doesn't let you stand anywhere. They give you a set spot to pick your ride, and you can be 100% guaranteed that your ride will be there, you know, unlike some human drivers. Plus Waymo has a better navigation system to get you to that specific spot. See, we've been paying Uber to improve on this for years now. The car arrived in about 10 minutes. You get to unlock the car from the app and once you start your ride, it welcomed me like we were about to launch into space and it genuinely felt futuristic. Now I've driven cars in different countries all over the world, but sitting alone inside a moving car with no one at the wheel is different. You have to take a second and just tell yourself that everything is fine. And honestly speaking, I would pick this a thousand times over some of the Uber experiences I've had in the past. In fact, cities like San Francisco are already moving towards robo-taxis becoming the norm. The logic is simple. Most road accidents come from humans and from my experience, it makes more sense to automate this thing. So fun fact, that's the only car with the driver's seat in the car. It was built there to ease us into what's coming. This is definitely not the end goal. The future version has no driver's seat at all. Every seat is a passenger seat. No wheel, just like being on a trip. I would rate my experience 9 over 10. I don't know why I removed one, but guys, welcome to the future.
