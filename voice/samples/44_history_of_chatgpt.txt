Topic: HISTORY OF CHATGPT

In 2015, a small research team quietly began a project that almost no one paid attention to. And
by 2022, that project stepped into the world and changed the internet forever with over 800
million users visiting it every week. That project was ChatGPT, a software that now processes
over 18 billion messages every week, a tool used by more than 1 million businesses across 200
countries. ChatGPT has made OpenAI to be the world's most valuable startup with a valuation
of $500 billion in just three years after it was released. This is a software that has directly or
indirectly influenced every internet user that is alive today. Now, here's the biggest lesson for
you if you're trying to build anything whatsoever that will have a lasting impact: there is no such
thing as overnight success. Great things rarely explode on day one. They are built quietly,
consistently, and over time, they take over everywhere.
