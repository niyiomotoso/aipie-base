If you have spent a lot of time on the internet recently, you would have come across the term AI
model, and I bet you really want to know what it means. I think one of the best ways to explain
things generally is using analogies. For example, if you can understand how birds fly, you are
very close to understanding how airplanes fly as well. To understand models, let's think of AI
itself as transportation. That said, AI models are simply like vehicles. In the world of AI, a model
is simply any system that you've trained to make predictions. So next time when you hear AI
models, just think of any car, like a Mercedes-Benz, like a Honda, like a Toyota. But then, what
are LLMs and why are they different from other models? LLM simply means large language
model. You know there are cars and there are cars. For example, Tesla Cybertruck. If you go to
Tesla today and you say you want a Tesla Cybertruck, you're basically saying you want a car
that is electric, that is luxurious, and can self-drive. And that's not every car you see on the road.
So it's a special car. That's the way LLMs are. They are special models. So in the AI world,
LLMs are models that have been trained on a large scale and more importantly, they are able to
generate human-like responses. So example would be ChatGPT models, DeepSeek models,
Gemini models, and the rest. So all Cybertrucks are cars, but not all cars are Cybertrucks. In the
same way, all LLMs are models, but not all models are LLMs. In terms of ownership, the way
Tesla owns Cybertruck and Volkswagen owns Lamborghini, that's exactly the same way OpenAI
owns ChatGPT models and Google owns Gemini models. So to summarize, think of AI
generally as transportation, models as the vehicles, and LLMs as special vehicles with the
ability to do certain things.
