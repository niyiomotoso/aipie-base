Topic: AI PLATORMS

Turn on this three AI platforms give you free access to ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok all in
one place. Number one, GlobalGPT.com. With one interface, you can switch between the
different AI models to generate text, videos, and images. The second one is AI Fiesta. This one
gives you side-by-side answers from all these different AI models. So, for example, you can see
the outputs from Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek side-by-side in one single chat. The third one is
ChatHub.gg. With this one, you can send your prompts for free to six different models all at the
same time.
