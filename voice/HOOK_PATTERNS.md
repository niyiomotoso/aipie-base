# Hook Patterns

> Extracted from 46 past scripts. These are Niyi's proven hook structures.

## Pattern 1: Bold Claim / News Drop
Start with a surprising fact, stat, or recent event.
- "Microsoft CEO recently said we have just one year to 18 months for AI to take over most office jobs."
- "ChatGPT just bought me this ice cream."
- "Google just solved the oldest problem in education."
- "ChatGPT officially now has an academy where you can learn how to properly use AI."
- "The update just dropped and most people think they need to update something. No, you don't."

## Pattern 2: "What if" / Hypothetical
Pose a thought-provoking scenario that makes the viewer lean in.
- "What if ChatGPT asked multiple AIs before it gave you an answer?"
- "What if I told you the AI tool you're using every day is about to change completely?"
- "What if the next big tech layoff isn't because of money — it's because of AI?"

## Pattern 3: "Imagine" / Visualization
Paint a picture that pulls the viewer into a future scenario.
- "Imagine waking up and your AI has already sorted your emails, booked your meetings, and written your reports."
- "Imagine a world where you never have to Google anything again."
- "Imagine your company replaces half its software tools with one AI agent."

## Pattern 4: "Did you know" / Surprising Fact
Lead with a stat or fact most people haven't heard yet.
- "Did you know ChatGPT now has over 400 million users? And most of them are using it wrong."
- "Did you know there's a free AI tool that can turn any document into a podcast?"
- "Did you know one AI company just turned down $200 million because of their principles?"

## Pattern 5: "If there is" / Conditional Setup
Set up a scenario that the viewer can relate to, then deliver the payoff.
- "If there is one AI skill you need to learn this year, it's this one."
- "If there is a tool that can actually replace Google for research, this is it."
- "If there is one thing that will decide whether your business survives the next 5 years, it's how you use AI."

## Pattern 6: Direct Challenge
Confront the viewer with a truth they might not want to hear.
- "Let me be very honest with you. You're never going to outperform AI in your field."
- "ChatGPT can lie to you, and if you're not careful, it will land you in big trouble."
- "ChatGPT has a secret code that would drastically change how it gives you answers. I know you've heard this before, but it's a lie."

## Pattern 7: Personal Story
Open with a first-person experience.
- "I took my first ever driverless car ride from downtown Los Angeles to West Hollywood."
- "I built a game for my son recently using AI, and it was super nice."
- "A few months ago, someone reached out to me from an organization."
- "I have made about 100 dollars in one week from just my voice."

## Pattern 8: List / Resource Intro
Promise a specific number of items.
- "These are the bare minimum AI skills every professional needs to survive 2026."
- "There are four AI learning platforms I will recommend to people who want to learn AI at its core."
- "Here are the four platforms that can lead a very solid foundation of coding and AI for your kids."
- "Three free AI courses I want you to take this weekend."

## Pattern 9: Problem/Pain Point
Start with a frustration the viewer relates to.
- "If you're tired of rewriting your CV and applying to random jobs every time, let me teach you something smarter."
- "If you're tired of ChatGPT sounding like a bot..."
- "If you struggle with problem solving or thinking through solutions, this AI can help you."
- "ChatGPT is responding slowly to your messages. Let me tell you why."
- "AI tools are way too expensive. Even as someone that builds AI systems every day, I completely agree."

## Pattern 10: "If you..." Qualifier
Target a specific audience upfront.
- "If you're a software engineer or a data scientist, or you write any form of code..."
- "If you're working in tech, listen carefully."
- "If you are planning to start a business or venture into something, then you need to hear this."

## Usage Notes
- Hooks should be 1-2 sentences max
- Get to the point immediately — no throat-clearing
- The hook should make the viewer think "I need to keep watching"
- Niyi often follows the hook with "Let me show you" or "Let me explain"
- **IMPORTANT: Vary the hook patterns across scripts.** When generating 3 hooks per script, use 3 DIFFERENT patterns. Across all scripts in a batch, ensure a MIX of patterns — don't default to Bold Claim for everything. Rotate through: Imagine, What if, Did you know, If there is, List, Challenge, etc.
