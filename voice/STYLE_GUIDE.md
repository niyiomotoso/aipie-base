## Who is Niyi?
Lead software engineer with experience building AI systems. Creates AI/tech
educational content for a non-technical audience — professionals, business
owners, students, and parents who want to understand and use AI practically.

## Tone & Personality
- **Conversational and direct** — talks TO the viewer, not AT them
- **Confident but not arrogant** — shares expertise without being preachy
- **Honest and grounded** — acknowledges nuance ("it comes with a lot of nuances, but hey")
- **Encouraging** — empowers viewers to take action, not fear AI
- **Occasionally humorous** — dry humor, self-aware ("I don't know why I removed one")
- **Nigerian cultural flavor** — natural references ("your village people", "Naira", "jollof rice")

## Language Patterns
- Uses "you" and "your" constantly — always addressing the viewer directly
- Second person dominant: "Let me show you", "Let me tell you", "Let me explain"
- Rhetorical questions to hook: "What if...", "You know how...", "Have you ever..."
- Transition phrases: "So here's the thing", "But here is where it gets tricky", "Now here's the interesting part"
- Simplifies with analogies: AI models = cars, LLMs = special cars, AI = food recipes
- Uses numbered lists within scripts: "Number one...", "Number two...", "The third one..."
- Casual contractions: "you're", "don't", "it's", "that's", "won't"
- Avoids jargon unless explaining it: always defines technical terms for non-technical viewers

## Sentence Structure
- Short, punchy sentences mixed with longer explanatory ones
- Often starts sentences with "So", "And", "But", "Now", "See"
- Uses em-dashes for asides: "you know, accountability, internal politics, risk, and some level of ambiguity"
- Frequently uses "you see" and "you know" as verbal bridges
- Active voice almost exclusively

## Signature Phrases
- "Let me show you how"
- "Let me tell you"
- "Let me be very honest with you"
- "You don't need to..."
- "If you want to..." / "If you're new here..."
- "Here is the good news"
- "Here is where it gets tricky"
- "See you in my next video"
- "Follow me for more"
- "Comment [keyword] and I'll send it to you"
- "Make sure you follow me"
- "And this is regardless of any profession"

## Things Niyi NEVER Does
- Never uses overly formal corporate language
- Never talks down to the audience
- Never uses clickbait without delivering substance
- Never forgets the practical takeaway — every script has actionable advice
- Never stays purely theoretical — always grounds concepts in real-world examples

## Script Length
- Short tips: 90-150 words (~30-45 seconds)
- Standard scripts: 150-250 words (~60-90 seconds)
- Deep explainers: 250-400 words (~90-120 seconds)
- Target for generated scripts: 150-200 words (~60 seconds)
